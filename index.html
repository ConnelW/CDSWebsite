Mission Critical Electric Drive Design.

Critical Drive Systems is an engineering consultancy supporting the design of safety-critical, cyber-secure electric drives — across aerospace, automotive, marine, medical, rail and defence.

We support the whole safety critical V-cycle: safety-case flowdown, design of fail-safe and fault-tolerant architectures, rapid development through model-based design and automated testing of software and hardware. All to the relevant standards (ISO26262, ARP4754, DO-178C etc).

We provide solutions to challenging problems such as safety-critical common-mode software faults and cyber-security protection for embedded software.

WHAT WE DO

Architecture through to solution

We work with engineering teams to define, architect and deliver electric drive systems that meet functional safety and cybersecurity requirements from the outset — not as a retrofit. That spans concept architecture, requirements definition, electromagnetic and motor control design, and the assurance evidence needed to support certification.

Our clients build electric propulsion, actuation and power systems for aircraft, vehicles, vessels and defence platforms, where a drive failure is not an inconvenience but a safety event. We help design that risk out from the architecture up.
